# Compliance Statement

_Last Updated: October 4, 2026_

Qorva is committed to handling personal data lawfully and transparently. This page states only what we do today. Qorva does not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification.

## Data Protection

- **GDPR:** We process candidate and user data on behalf of our customers, who remain the data controllers. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available on request.
- **Data deletion:** Candidates and jobs can be deleted at any time from the application, along with their related data. Company accounts are deleted on request.
- **No selling of data:** Candidate data is never sold or shared with third parties, except the subprocessors listed on our [Security](/security) page.

## Accessibility

We aim to make our website and application accessible to everyone. See our [Accessibility](/accessibility) statement.
